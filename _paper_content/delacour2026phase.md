The spiked mixture model generalizes the classical spiked covariance framework to a mixture of subpopulations sharing random high-dimensional signal directions. We characterize sharp transitions in the extreme eigenvalues as a function of spike strengths, mixture probabilities, correlations, and the aspect ratio. These transitions provide quantitative criteria for detecting signals from the empirical covariance spectrum.
